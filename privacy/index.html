Privacy

Newsletter subscriptions.

When you subscribe, your email address is sent to Buttondown, which stores subscriber emails and handles confirmation, delivery and unsubscribe requests. Confirm your address to activate your subscription. Unsubscribe using the link in an email. Read Buttondown’s privacy policy.

Practice test progress.

Practice tests store your answers, flags, timing and recent practice history on this device using browser storage. No test results are sent to SQE Daily or Buttondown. Other people using the same browser may be able to see saved attempts. Starting a new test or discarding a saved test clears the previous attempt. Clearing browser site data removes saved attempts and recent practice history.

Website analytics.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is configured but disabled until a site token is added. When enabled, it collects cookie-free website usage and performance measurements. We do not add advertising trackers. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Hosting and links.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Hosting providers may process technical request information to serve and protect their services. Legal-source links take you to external websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Contact and your information.

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, email hello@sqedaily.co.uk. SQE Daily does not sell your subscriber email address or share it with sponsors.
