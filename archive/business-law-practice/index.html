SQE1 Business Law & Practice questions

One question. One rule to remember. Browse the published practice.

Business law and practice is assessed in FLK1. It covers how businesses are set up and run: sole traders, partnerships, limited liability partnerships and companies; decision-making by directors and shareholders; business finance; insolvency; and the taxation of businesses and their owners. Much of it comes from statute, so the key primary sources are the Companies Act 2006, the Insolvency Act 1986, the Partnership Act 1890 and the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000. Questions often turn on a procedural step, such as which resolution is needed or what notice must be given. Every question below links to the source behind its answer.

Rules to remember
