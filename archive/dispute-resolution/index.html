SQE1 Dispute Resolution practice questions

One question. One rule to remember. Browse the published practice.

Dispute resolution is assessed in FLK1. It follows a civil claim from the first letter to enforcement: limitation, pre-action conduct, starting and serving proceedings, statements of case, case management, evidence, costs, trial and appeals, along with the choice between litigation and other ways of resolving disputes. The Civil Procedure Rules are the core source, with the Limitation Act 1980 and the Arbitration Act 1996. Questions often turn on a time limit or the next procedural step, so read the scenario's dates closely. Every question below links to the source behind its answer.

Rules to remember
