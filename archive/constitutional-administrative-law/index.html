Constitutional and administrative law is assessed in FLK1 as part of the legal system. It covers the institutions of the state, parliamentary sovereignty, the rule of law and the separation of powers, the royal prerogative, human rights, judicial review of public decisions, and EU law and its legacy in UK law. Key primary sources include the Human Rights Act 1998, the Senior Courts Act 1981, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 and Part 54 of the Civil Procedure Rules, which governs judicial review procedure.
