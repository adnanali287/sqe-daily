## Recommended selection rules (our design)
1. **Eligibility:** library-available, approved questions only. Never show held or unapproved drafts.
2. **No repeats within a test.** Across attempts, prefer questions the user has not seen recently, where local history exists.
3. **Mixed paper tests:** spread questions across the paper's subject groups in proportion to our mock allocation, rounding so that each subject appears at least once in a 20-question test. Ethics is about 8% (1 or 2 questions in a 20).
4. **Session tests:** draw only from that session's SRA subject grouping, the same as the real session.
5. **Difficulty:** aim for about 25% easy, 55% medium and 20% hard, matching each pool's own mix. Fall back to any difficulty rather than drop a subject. Labels are editorial, so never present them as predictive.
6. **Answer letters:** avoid four or more consecutive questions with the same correct letter, where the pool allows.
7. **Timing (optional):** 108 seconds per question mirrors the real pace (153 min ÷ 85). That is 18 min for 10 questions and 36 min for 20.
8. **Wording:** call these "practice tests". Never say "official", "real exam standard" or "predicted score".
