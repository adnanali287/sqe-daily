Ethics is 14 questions per paper (8.2%), well under the SRA's 20% ceiling. Ethics-classified questions count separately from their context subject, so subject percentages exclude them.

Editorial difficulty target: about 25% easy, 55% medium and 20% hard. These are our labels; we make no claim about the real exam's statistics.

Scenario variety: client advice, a procedural next step, identifying the rule, applying it to an outcome, and a calculation where the subject has one (tax, intestacy, accounts).
