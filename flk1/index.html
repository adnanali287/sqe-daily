SQE1 · FLK1

FLK1 practice questions

FLK1, Functioning Legal Knowledge 1, is the first of the two SQE1 assessments. It covers business law and practice, dispute resolution, contract, tort, the legal system of England and Wales (including constitutional and administrative law and EU law), and legal services. Ethics and professional conduct can be tested throughout.

How FLK1 is set

From January 2027, FLK1 has 170 single best answer questions, answered in two sessions of 85 questions, each lasting 2 hours 33 minutes. The SRA splits the subjects between the sessions: business law and practice, dispute resolution and legal services in the first, and contract, tort and the legal system in the second. Within each session the questions come in a mixed order. The SRA's announcement has the detail.

Using this page

Start with the subject you find hardest. Each subject page lists our published questions with the rules to remember, and every question links to the source behind its answer.

Dates

For the January 2027 sitting, FLK1 runs from 11 to 15 January 2027. See SQE1 exam dates for booking and results.
