Free SQE1 practice questions

SQE Daily publishes one original SQE1 practice question every day, with a worked answer, the rule to remember and a link to the source behind the answer. This page brings the questions together by paper and subject, so you can practise the areas you need most.

How the questions work

SQE1 uses single best answer questions: a short scenario, a question and five options. Several options may look plausible, but one is the best answer. Our questions follow the same format. Choose an answer to see whether you were right, then read why each option is right or wrong. Our guide to answering single best answer questions sets out a method you can use on every question.

Practise little and often

A question takes a few minutes. Short, regular practice spreads your revision out and keeps earlier subjects fresh. Come back to the site each day, or get tomorrow’s question by email.

Learn from every option

Knowing why the wrong options are wrong is as useful as finding the right one. If you miss a question, read the linked source, note the rule to remember, and try the other questions in that subject.
