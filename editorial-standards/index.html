Editorial standards

SQE Daily publishes one free SQE1 practice question a day. This page explains how those questions are put together, what the dates on the site mean, and how to tell us about a mistake.

Original questions

Every question is written for SQE Daily in the single best answer format the SRA uses for SQE1: a short scenario, a question and five options, one of which is the best answer. Each question is assigned to an SQE1 subject and to FLK1 or FLK2, following the SRA's SQE1 assessment specification. We do not reproduce questions from the SRA or from training providers. Each question is checked against the source it relies on before it is scheduled for publication.

Every answer links to its source

Each worked answer is linked to the source it relies on: usually legislation, a court judgment, rules of court procedure or the SRA’s own rules. Where no official copy of a source is freely available online, we link the most reliable public copy we can find. The source is named and linked beside every answer, so you can read it for yourself. The explanation sets out why the correct option is right and why each of the others is wrong, and a single rule to remember sums up the point being tested.

What our checks are, and are not

Our checks compare each question with the wording of its linked source. Questions are not reviewed by a practising solicitor or barrister, and they are not a substitute for your course materials. If an answer and its source seem to disagree, rely on the source and tell us.

What the dates mean

Each question shows the date it was published. A “Checked against primary source” date appears only when a question has actually been checked against its source, and shows when that check happened. When we change the content of a published question, the change appears on the same page and web address, and the date of the change is recorded for search engines.

Corrections

If you think an answer is wrong, out of date or unclear, use the “Report an error” link under any worked answer, or email hello@sqedaily.co.uk. Tell us which question and what you think is wrong. Confirmed errors are corrected on the question’s existing page.

The law at a point in time

The law changes. Each question reflects the law as stated in its linked source when the question was written. If you notice a change in the law that affects a question, please let us know.

Independent

SQE Daily is independent. It is not affiliated with the SRA or any training provider and it sells no course. Sponsorship does not decide what we publish. Questions are for revision practice only and are not legal advice.
